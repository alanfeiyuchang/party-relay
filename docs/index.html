常见问题

这个 App 怎么玩？

把在场的人分成两队，一部手机轮流传递。每轮转动转盘决定玩法，两队依次挑战同一个玩法，在限时内猜对更多词的一队赢下本轮。全部玩法都在一部手机上完成，不需要额外道具。

都有哪些玩法？

  • 🗣️ 你说我猜——用语言描述词语（不能说出词语本身），队友抢答。
  • 🎨 你画我猜——在画板上画出词语（不能写字、数字），队友根据画面猜。
  • 🤐 唇语猜词——不出声，只靠口型让队友读出词语。
  • 🕺 肢体模仿——只用动作和表情表演，不能发出声音。
  • 😜 表情管理——用 emoji 拼出词语，队友根据 emoji 猜。
  • 🌟 名人堂——两队各自被分配一个知名人物或虚构角色的名字，轮流互相提问，先猜中对方名字的一队获胜。

需要联网或者注册账号吗？

都不需要。派对接力完全离线运行，没有账号、没有广告、没有内购，也不会发起任何联网请求。

App 会收集我的数据吗？

不会。所有内容（队伍名称、设置、语言偏好）都只保存在你自己的设备上，不会上传到任何服务器。详见下方隐私政策。

怎么切换中文 / 英文？

在首页右上角点击语言按钮即可随时切换，两种语言的词库都是各自独立手写的，不是机器翻译。

怎么修改队伍名称、轮数或每轮时长？

在首页点击「设置」，可以调整队伍名称与表情、总轮数、每轮时长、震动和音效等。

防偷看模式是什么？

这是一个可选功能：开启后，手机立起来才显示词语，放平会自动隐藏，方便在人群中传递手机时不被提前看到答案。可以在「设置」里关闭。

我发现词库里有错别字 / 有词不合适

欢迎邮件告诉我们具体是哪一个词，我们会在下一个版本里修正或移除。

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

Split everyone into two teams and pass one phone back and forth. Each round, spin the wheel to pick a game; both teams play the same game one after the other, and whoever guesses more words before the timer runs out wins the round. Everything happens on a single phone — no extra equipment needed.

What games are included?

  • 🗣️ Say & Guess — describe the word out loud without saying the word itself.
  • 🎨 Draw & Guess — sketch it on the in-app canvas; no letters or numbers.
  • 🤐 Lip Reading — mouth the word silently and let your team read your lips.
  • 🕺 Charades — act it out with gestures alone.
  • 😜 Emoji Manager — spell the word out in emoji and let your team decode it.
  • 🌟 Hall of Fame — each team is secretly assigned the name of a well-known person or fictional character; teams take turns asking questions until one guesses the other's name.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

Neither. PartyRelay runs fully offline — no sign-up, no ads, no in-app purchases, and no network requests of any kind.

Does the app collect my data?

No. Everything (team names, settings, language preference) is stored only on your own device and never uploaded. See the privacy policy below.

How do I switch between English and Chinese?

Tap the language button in the top-right corner of the home screen. Word lists are written natively for each language rather than machine-translated.

How do I change team names, round count, or turn length?

Tap Settings on the home screen to adjust team names and emoji, number of rounds, time per turn, haptics, and sound.

What is the privacy guard?

An optional feature: with it on, the word only appears while the phone is held upright and hides automatically when the phone is laid flat, so nobody sees the answer early while the phone is being passed around. You can turn it off in Settings.

I found a typo or a word that doesn't belong

Email us the exact word and we will correct or remove it in the next update.
