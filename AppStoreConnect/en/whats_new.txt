FOLDED PHONES
Fold the phone to 90° and stand it on the table, and one phone gives three people their own view. The top half faces you with the word and the timer. The bottom half lies flat as the buttons and the drawing canvas, right under your thumb. The outer screen faces everyone else with the timer and the score — and in Draw & Guess it mirrors the whole drawing, live.

The first time you fold it, a short card explains what each screen is for. You can bring it back any time from Settings.

CLOSE IT TO HIDE THE WORD
Shut the phone mid-round and the word is hidden and the clock stops. Closing it is how you hand it over, so whoever picks it up next doesn't see the answer first.

DRAW & GUESS, FOLDED
Folded, the word and the canvas are on screen together — no more tapping "Start drawing" back and forth. Folding or unfolding mid-drawing no longer loses the strokes.

ABOUT THE CAMERA PERMISSION
iOS only lets an app put content on the outer screen while it is using the camera, so folded play asks for camera access. The app never photographs, records, saves, or displays anything from the camera. Decline it and everything else works as before — only the outer screen stays dark. A non-folding iPhone is never asked for the camera.
