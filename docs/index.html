常见问题

这个 App 怎么玩？

打开 App 先选一个模式，全部玩法都在一部手机上完成，不需要额外道具：

  • ⚔️ 对战模式——把在场的人分成两队，一部手机轮流传递。每轮转动转盘决定玩法，两队依次挑战同一个玩法，在限时内猜对更多词的一队赢下本轮。
  • ⚡️ 快速模式——不分队、不记分，转一次盘只打一局。
  • 🔄 轮流模式——每个人轮流表演、各记各分，谁猜对就点谁的名字。

都有哪些玩法？

  • 🗣️ 你说我猜——用语言描述词语（不能说出词语本身），队友抢答。
  • 🎨 你画我猜——在画板上画出词语（不能写字、数字），队友根据画面猜。
  • 🤐 唇语猜词——不出声，只靠口型让队友读出词语。
  • 🕺 肢体模仿——只用动作和表情表演，不能发出声音。
  • 🌟 名人堂——两队各自被分配一个知名人物或虚构角色的名字，轮流互相提问，先猜中对方名字的一队获胜。（只在对战模式里出现）

需要联网或者注册账号吗？

都不需要。派对接力完全离线运行，没有账号、没有广告、没有内购，也不会发起任何联网请求。

App 会收集我的数据吗？

不会。所有内容（队伍名称、玩家名字和头像、设置、语言偏好）都只保存在你自己的设备上，不会上传到任何服务器。详见下方隐私政策。

怎么切换中文 / 英文？

点击右上角的语言按钮即可随时切换，两种语言的词库都是各自独立手写的，不是机器翻译。

人不够分两队怎么办？

在主页选快速模式：不分队、不记分，「开始游戏」转一次盘只打一局，打完直接看这一局猜对了几个。或者选轮流模式，每个人各记各分。

轮流模式怎么玩？

先定人数，点玩家可以改名字、换头像或删除，长按拖动可以调整顺序。开局随机抽一个人先来，之后按顺序轮流：轮到谁，谁就转盘选玩法、负责表演，其他人一起猜。屏幕上没有「猜对」按钮，其他人的名字围在词的周围，谁猜对了就点谁，表演者和猜对的人各加 1 分。所有人都表演过一次后会显示排行榜。

点错了 / 想重来这一局怎么办？

点游戏页左上角的「暂停」，计时会停下。可以选「重开小局」（这一小局的得分清零、换一批新词重新来），也可以「结束游戏」或「继续」。

怎么修改队伍名称、轮数或每轮时长？

队伍名称和玩家名字在选好模式后的准备页上直接修改；总轮数、每轮时长、震动和音效等在「设置」里调整。

防偷看模式是什么？

这是一个可选功能：开启后，手机立起来才显示词语，放平会自动隐藏，方便在人群中传递手机时不被提前看到答案。可以在「设置」里关闭。

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

Pick a mode when the app opens. Everything happens on a single phone — no extra equipment needed:

  • ⚔️ Team Battle — split everyone into two teams and pass one phone back and forth. Each round, spin the wheel to pick a game; both teams play the same game one after the other, and whoever guesses more words before the timer runs out wins the round.
  • ⚡️ Quick Mode — no teams, no scores: one spin, one round.
  • 🔄 Take Turns — everyone takes a turn giving clues and scores for themselves; tap whoever guessed.

What games are included?

  • 🗣️ Say & Guess — describe the word out loud without saying the word itself.
  • 🎨 Draw & Guess — sketch it on the in-app canvas; no letters or numbers.
  • 🤐 Lip Reading — mouth the word silently and let your team read your lips.
  • 🕺 Charades — act it out with gestures alone.
  • 🌟 Hall of Fame — each team is secretly assigned the name of a well-known person or fictional character; teams take turns asking questions until one guesses the other's name. (Team Battle only.)

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

Neither. PartyRelay runs fully offline — no sign-up, no ads, no in-app purchases, and no network requests of any kind.

Does the app collect my data?

No. Everything (team names, player names and avatars, settings, language preference) is stored only on your own device and never uploaded. See the privacy policy below.

How do I switch between English and Chinese?

Tap the language button in the top-right corner. Word lists are written natively for each language rather than machine-translated.

What if we don't have enough people for two teams?

Pick Quick Mode on the home screen: no teams, no scores — Start Game spins once, plays a single round, and shows how many words you got. Or pick Take Turns, where everyone scores for themselves.

How does Take Turns work?

Set the number of players, tap anyone to change their name or avatar or remove them, and long-press to drag them into order. A random player goes first, then it goes round in order: on your turn you spin for a game and give the clues while everyone else guesses. There's no "got it" button — the other players' names surround the word, and tapping whoever guessed gives a point to them and to you. After everyone has had a turn, a leaderboard shows the totals.

Someone mis-tapped — can we redo the turn?

Tap Pause in the top-left corner of the game screen; the clock stops. Choose Restart this turn (that turn's points are cleared and you get fresh words), End game, or Resume.

How do I change team names, round count, or turn length?

Team and player names are edited right on the setup page after you pick a mode; number of rounds, time per turn, haptics, and sound are in Settings.

What is the privacy guard?

An optional feature: with it on, the word only appears while the phone is held upright and hides automatically when the phone is laid flat, so nobody sees the answer early while the phone is being passed around. You can turn it off in Settings.

I found a typo or a word that doesn't belong

Email us the exact word and we will correct or remove it in the next update.
