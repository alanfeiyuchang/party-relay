Marketing URL (optional): https://github.com/alanfeiyuchang/party-relay
Privacy Policy URL: https://alanfeiyuchang.github.io/party-relay/#privacy

The Support URL is served by GitHub Pages from the `docs/` folder on the `main` branch
(source file: docs/index.html). It contains a contact email with a stated response time,
a FAQ, and the privacy policy — which is what Apple looks for under guideline 1.5.

Do NOT use https://github.com/alanfeiyuchang/party-relay/issues as the Support URL:
that was rejected in the 1.0 (4) review (guideline 1.5) because an issue tracker is not
a support page for end users.

To publish: GitHub repo -> Settings -> Pages -> Source: "Deploy from a branch",
Branch: main, Folder: /docs -> Save. Wait ~1 minute, then confirm the URL loads.
